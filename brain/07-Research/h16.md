# h16

**Event-driven strategies, one type at a time.** Twelve event types, each held 90 sessions
after a high-materiality filing. Pre-registered in `hypotheses/h16_event_strategies.toml`
with three ranked priors.

All twelve REJECT, and two of the three priors held:

* **Prior 1 held** - the negative types are avoidance signals. CLARIFICATION reaches
  **t = -6.73** across 191 names, the strongest statistic of either sign this project has
  produced, failing only cost and DSR. You cannot profit by buying it; you can by not
  buying it.
* **Prior 2 held** - the positives die on capacity. JOINT_VENTURE has the best net
  (+0.0417%) and passes the cost gate, on ten names held.
* **Prior 3 was wrong in its bet, right in its reasoning.** PROMOTER_PLEDGE was named as
  most likely to pass and came in at t = -2.89. But the mechanism the prior gave -
  pledging signals promoter stress - predicts exactly that negative sign. The reasoning
  and the bet pointed in opposite directions, which is a distinct kind of error worth
  recording.

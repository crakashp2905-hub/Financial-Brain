# h15

**Opening-range breakout systems on minute bars.** Pre-registered in
`hypotheses/h15_intraday_breakout.toml` with an explicit, confident prior that all four
would fail, and the arithmetic for why stated in advance.

    opening_range         fires 60%   gross +0.043%   net -0.666%   t -91.69
    dual_thrust           fires 17%   gross +0.096%   net -0.613%   t -40.03
    r_breaker             fires  9%   gross +0.060%   net -0.649%   t -30.44
    dynamic_breakout_ii   fires  7%   gross +0.028%   net -0.681%   t -26.19

The prior held. Cost is **7x to 25x** the gross edge.

Two things worth keeping: the edge is **real** (every system is gross-positive, so the
opening-range effect exists here), and the elaborate systems genuinely **beat the plain
reference** on a per-trade basis - dual_thrust more than doubles it. They work; the market
they were designed for is futures, not Indian cash equity.

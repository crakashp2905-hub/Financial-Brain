# h11

**The 200-day trend filter on a tradeable universe.** Pre-registered in
`hypotheses/h11_trend_tradeable_universe.toml` before running, with three possible
outcomes ranked by expectation and success criteria fixed in advance.

    REJECT  trial 58  IC +0.0236  t +1.80  net -0.102%/period  turnover 77%
    gates:  significance FAIL, costs FAIL, deflated_sharpe FAIL,
            walk_forward PASS, regime PASS, capacity PASS

**Outcome 1 fired** - the one written down as most likely. The signal roughly halved
(t +3.69 to +1.80) when the universe was restricted to the most liquid 15%, because trend
is stronger in smaller names and the restriction removes exactly those. Cheaper costs did
not compensate; the gross fell further than the cost did.

Capacity passed, so this is a real failure rather than a technicality.

# h14

**Stand down when India VIX is high.** Pre-registered in `hypotheses/h14_vix_regime.toml`
with a genuinely uncertain prior - avoided drawdowns against missed rebounds - and the
mechanism that would decide it stated in advance.

    trend_calm_vix  REJECT  trial 62  t -2.85  net -0.1618%/session  invested 81%

The rebounds won. Invested only 81% of sessions, the rule earns cash while the benchmark
earns the market, and in a right-skewed universe that is where the returns are. A
market-wide gate also flips the entire book at once, tripling turnover.

India VIX had been in `index_levels` since 2015 - 2,894 sessions - and had never been used
as a feature. It is wired in now, and the archive records that using it as an on/off switch
for a whole book is a bad design. Whether it has value as a *sizing* input is untested.

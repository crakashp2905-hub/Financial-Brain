# Result of indicator consensus vote

Pre-registration: [[PRE-REGISTERED indicator consensus vote]]. Each of the 161 library features voted with the
direction it had over 2016-2021; names ranked by the mean vote; scored like the learned model.

| 2022-2026, net a month over the 500 most liquid (t) | top 20 | top 50 |
|---|---|---|
| **consensus vote of all 161** | **-0.38% (-1.1)** | -0.31% (-1.3) |
| learned model (the same 161, weights learned) | +0.77% (2.3) | +0.59% (2.3) |
| momentum | +0.48% (0.9) | +0.53% (1.4) |
| consensus minus learned | -1.15% (t -2.3) | -0.91% (t -2.3) |

**C1: right. It fails** and does not join the books. An equal vote of every indicator loses money: most of the 161
carry their information in the bottom of the ranking (volatile, recently-spiked names doing worse), so averaging them
picks calm names that do not outperform, and the few signals that pay long-only are drowned. Using every indicator
works only when the weights are learned and tested, which is what the learned model does. The vote is still shown on
every chart as information - how many indicators agree - never as a reason to trade.

# h12

**Slow trend at low turnover: a 5% hysteresis band on the 200-day filter.** Pre-registered
in `hypotheses/h12_low_turnover_trend.toml` with one parameter chosen once, three ranked
outcomes, and success criteria fixed before running. Declared in advance to be the **last**
pre-registered test of slow trend.

    REJECT  trial 59  t -0.45  net -0.0026%/session  turnover 1.154%/session
    gates:  significance FAIL, costs FAIL, deflated_sharpe FAIL,
            walk_forward FAIL (36% of years), regime FAIL, capacity PASS

**Outcome 2.** The band worked - turnover fell roughly threefold, from ~70% a period to
~23% - and the signal died with it, t collapsing from the golden cross's +2.74 to -0.45.

**The finding:** the slow-trend signal and the turnover it generates are the same thing.
A raw close-vs-SMA200 rule earns its t = +3.69 *at the crossing*; delaying entry and exit
by 5% to dodge the churn removes exactly the part carrying the information.

Slow trend is real, lives in illiquid names ([[h11]]), is inseparable from its turnover,
and is now closed. See [[Slow trend is closed]].

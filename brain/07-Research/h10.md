# h10

**Candlestick patterns as entry signals.** Seven patterns held 20 sessions, pre-registered
in `hypotheses/h10_candlestick_patterns.toml` with a weak negative prior and two
diagnostics built in: `doji` as a directionless control, and `marubozu_bull` as a
circuit-contamination check.

All seven REJECT. The control fired at t = -13.60, the harness was investigated before the
result was believed, and what that found - a volatility confound for six of seven, and a
structural explanation proposed then refuted by a random-basket control - is in
[[Charts, candles and indicators]].

# Regulatory gates

Three distinct gates, often conflated.

1. **Algorithmic order placement** — SEBI's retail-algo framework routes algo orders
   through the broker with registration/tagging and treats flow above a per-second
   threshold as algorithmic. [[Kite Connect]] additionally requires a static IP.
   Personal, low-frequency, human-approved placement is the easy path.
2. **Investment advice** — the moment recommendations reach anyone but you, SEBI's
   Research Analyst / Investment Adviser regulations apply. This is the hard gate on the
   product ambition; it is a licensing question, not an engineering one.
3. **Data redistribution** — showing licensed exchange data to third parties requires an
   exchange agreement.

> [!warning] Verify current circulars before acting. These have been revised repeatedly
> and nothing here is legal advice.

Net: personal-use Financial-Brain is unblocked. Product Financial-Brain has a regulatory
precondition. See [[D1 Personal tool or product]].

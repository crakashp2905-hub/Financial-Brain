# Promoter group intelligence

The India-specific payoff the US market does not offer, and it is graph-shaped:

- promoter group structures across listed entities
- **pledging** — shares pledged against borrowings, a live distress signal
- related-party transactions
- **group contagion** — one group's stress propagating across its listed companies

Genuinely underbuilt by existing products. Part of [[C24 Knowledge Graph]] and a
candidate for the project's real edge.

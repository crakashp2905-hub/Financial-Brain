# Multiple testing in a small universe

~2,000 listed names, ~500 genuinely liquid, and short clean history. Compared with US or
China A-share research, false positives are far likelier per hypothesis tested.

Deflated Sharpe and multiple-testing control are therefore **not optional** — see
[[Alpha Validation Firewall]].

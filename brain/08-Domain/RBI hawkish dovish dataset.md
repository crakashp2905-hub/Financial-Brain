# RBI hawkish dovish dataset

The Indian analogue of the FOMC "Trillion Dollar Words" task: classify [[RBI]] policy
statements and minutes as hawkish / dovish / neutral.

Highest value and most tractable of the datasets in [[C21 India evaluation benchmark]].
Source is free, volume is manageable, and it feeds [[C07 Market Regime Brain]] directly.
Build first.

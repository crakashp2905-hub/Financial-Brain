# Point-in-time fundamentals

> [!danger] The single biggest blocker
> No affordable Indian source supplies point-in-time fundamentals.

[[Screener]], [[Moneycontrol]] and most aggregators serve **restated, as-of-today**
financials. Consequences:

- restatements are folded back into history
- the *reporting date* is usually absent; only the *period end*. In India the gap is
  routinely 30–60 days
- ratios are recomputed with today's share count after splits and bonuses

So [[Fundamental-Factor-Mining]]-style work cannot be validated on Indian history. Any
Sharpe produced there is fiction.

## Mitigations, in order of value
1. **Start our own PIT store today** — [[C03 PIT fundamentals store]]
2. Key fundamentals to **filing timestamps** from [[NSE BSE announcements]]
3. Buy [[CMIE Prowess]] if this becomes commercial
4. Until then restrict to slow-moving, restatement-resistant quantities with a
   deliberate 90-day lag

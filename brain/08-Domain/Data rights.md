# Data rights

Public accessibility != the right to scrape, store, redistribute or commercialise.

- [[NSE]] has explicit policies on use, display, distribution and redistribution;
  commercial use needs an agreement. Corporate-data subscription quoted around
  ₹10.6 lakh/year
- [[NSE MCP interface]] is stated as educational/informational — **not** a production feed
- [[Screener]] and [[Moneycontrol]] terms are unclear; prefer official feeds

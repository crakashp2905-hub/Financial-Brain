# Strategy Genome

Strategies are not isolated `strategy_001.py` files. They are combinations of primitives:

**Universe · Signal · Confirmation · Regime filter · Entry · Position sizing · Exit ·
Risk management**

This lets the system recombine and discover rather than hand-code, and lets
[[C25 Outcome attribution and calibration]] attribute results to specific genes.

# Scores are not predictions

A score is meaningful only if it declares: universe and horizon; benchmark and definition
of success; point-in-time features and missing-data policy; cost, liquidity and capacity
assumptions; historical calibration by score band, regime and market-cap segment; and
uncertainty, data quality and disagreement reported **separately** from the score.

Never present `82/100` as a probability of a price move. It is a decision aid whose
usefulness is measured against a transparent benchmark.

Entry bands (80–100 strong · 70–79 watch · 60–69 wait · 40–59 avoid · <40 strong avoid)
must be **learned and backtested**, never hand-chosen.

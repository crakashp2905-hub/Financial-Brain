# Source hierarchy

When sources disagree, **Tier 1 wins.**

| Tier | Sources |
|---|---|
| 1 | [[NSE]], [[BSE]], [[Company filings]], annual reports, investor presentations, regulatory filings |
| 2 | [[Kite Connect]] market data, official company announcements |
| 3 | [[Screener]], [[Moneycontrol]], Economic Times, Business Standard, Reuters |
| 4 | news, social media, forums |

Matters more in India than the US: Screener and Moneycontrol diverge from filings more
often than US aggregators diverge from EDGAR.

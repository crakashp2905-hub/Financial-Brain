# Portfolio Brain

Should we deploy it, how much, and what is the risk?

**Portfolio-aware selection:** Strategy A at Sharpe 1.6 with 0.85 correlation to the
existing portfolio is worth less than Strategy B at Sharpe 1.4 with 0.15 correlation.
Always evaluate at portfolio level: correlation, factor exposure, drawdown, tail risk.

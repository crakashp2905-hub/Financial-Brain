# The control

Any measurement of a picker needs the thing it picked *from*.

The [[C16 Investment Committee]] only ever sees companies that filed something material
that session. That universe has its own return. Without pricing it:

- a good result gets claimed for the committee when it belonged to the market;
- a bad one gets blamed on the committee when the universe was falling.

So every candidate a session surfaces is recorded - drafted or not - and priced exactly
as a paper trade is. `fb control` reports selected against blind, and their difference.

The first run of this was decisive: the committee returned +3.15% per trade and the same
universe bought blindly returned +5.00%, a contribution of **-1.85%**. See
[[Twenty-five trades and no edge]].

This is the cheapest possible guard against the most common self-deception in systematic
investing, and it cost one table and one query.

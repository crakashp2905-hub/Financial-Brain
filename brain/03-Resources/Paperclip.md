**Agent control plane — parked until later phases**

Roles, reporting lines, budgets, goals, tasks, delegation, auditability; agents woken by
schedules, assignments or mentions, running short heartbeat cycles.

Compelling for the research-organisation model, **but** a large agent hierarchy is
explicitly not needed for the first product. See [[D5 Paperclip now or later]].

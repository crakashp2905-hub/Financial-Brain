## 2. Momentum as net excess, with its null (`momentum_excess.py`)

Recomputed unchanged from the registration, a holding with no month-end price scored at its last traded price.

| book | net excess / month | cost / month | turnover | t (HAC) | halves t | null q95 (full t) | P(real), family 20 | tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 plain 12-1 momentum | +1.28% | 0.20% | 40% | 2.73 | 2.28 / 1.76 | 0.65 | 0.52 | INCUBATE |
| P3 frog in the pan | +1.44% | 0.20% | 42% | 3.15 | 3.33 / 1.35 | 1.07 | 0.77 | CANDIDATE_SMALL |

Both beat their random-book null. With P3's 22 live zoo sessions folded in (z -0.37) the posterior is 0.75.

What tempers it:

* **It has weakened.** Second-half t is 1.35; the last 36 months give t 0.67; 2022 and 2024 were negative years
  (-1% and -18% excess).
* **Excess drawdown 26%**, worst rolling 12 months -24% against the universe. It is a concentrated, high-turnover
  book and behaves like one.
* **P3 is the best of about twenty momentum variants**, and the more elaborate M1 scores lower. The family
  adjustment charges for that; it cannot prove the ranking among variants is real.
* **Fixed charges.** The cost model is proportional. The depository charge (about Rs 16 per scrip sold) adds
  3.2% a year at Rs 50,000, 0.8% at Rs 2 lakh and 0.16% at Rs 10 lakh, and whole-share rounding is not modelled.
* **Tax.** Monthly turnover makes every gain short-term (the tax study measured a fifth of pre-tax CAGR).

## 3. One combination (`combo_momentum_carry.py`)

P3 momentum plus C1 rich-basis carry, inverse-volatility weights: correlation 0.01, t 2.86 (HAC 2.60), halves
**2.45 / 3.04**, posterior 0.64, INCUBATE. It is the first series in the project with t above 2 in both halves,
but it was assembled after both parts were known, it has no null, and the carry leg needs a full futures lot of
spot plus margin, which a small account cannot hold.

## What follows

1. P3 is the one book to carry forward. It is already in the zoo (`edge_p3_frog_in_pan`, live since 2026-10-08),
   so its forward clock is running. It needs a registration under ADR-0007 (prior A, family 20) if the owner
   accepts the ADR.
2. N2 and A13 are avoid filters with strong evidence. M1's N2 exclusion moved it by -0.01pp, so they matter for
   a broad book or for discretionary buying, not for a 20-name momentum book.
3. Nothing intraday, nothing in options premium, nothing technical clears any version of the bar.

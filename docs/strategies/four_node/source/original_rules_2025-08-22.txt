2025 0822 0930 One Minute - 4 Node Strategy

Indicators Required:
1. Smoothed MA 20 Low (component of 20C)
2. Smoothed MA 20 Closed (component of 20C)
3. Smoothed MA 20 High (component of 20C)
4. Smoothed MA 8 Closed
5. Smoothed MA 50 Closed
6. Smoothed MA 100 Closed
7. Smoothed MA 200 Closed
8. Fractal Indicator 

Objects Required:
1. Fibonacci: Daily (Levels: -323.4, -200.0, -161.8, -141.4, -100, -76.4, -61.8, -41.4, -27.0, -12.8, 0.0, 11.4, 23.6, 38.2, 50.0, 61.8, 76.4, 88.6, 100.0, 112.8, 127.0, 141.4, 161.8, 176.4, 200.0, 261.8, 423.6).

Script: XAUUSD
Timeframe: 1 Minute
 
"20 Cluster" or "20C" is the combination of Points 1, 2 and 3 in the Indicator list.

Wait for 3 trending fractal nodes in zigzag fashion (N1, N2, N3) to appear permanently.
Enter Sell or Buy, when price (M1 Candle) close below or above N2 (below for Sell and above for Buy).

Lot size:"X" times the balance (Value should be entered during initiation). When X=1, then lot size should be 0.01 for balance of $100 and when X=3, then lot size should be 0.03 for $100.

Sell condn:
-----------
N1 should be equal or above 20C High.
N3 should equal or less than 20C Mid.
Exception: If N3 is above or equal to N1, enter when price closes below N2 without a return candle (return candle is a candle in opposite direction).

Buy condn:
----------
N1 should be equal or below 20C Low.
N3 should equal or less than 20C Mid.
Exception: If N3 is above or equal to N1, enter when price closes below N2 without a return candle.

Stop Loss: 
---------
Set SL above or below N3 with value +/- Spread of the broker for the chosen script (Above for Sell and Below for Buy).

Take Profit :
-----------
Set TP 20 Pips upon entry.
Don't enter (Buy or Sell) if SL is more than 15 Pips.
 
Progressive movement of Price and SL:
-------------------------------------
Once the price moves in the positive direction and when the profit touches 10 Pips, remove the TP and move SL to 5 Pips from the entry point. As the price keeps moving every 5 Pips in the positive direction, Keep progressively moving the SL to 5 Pips as 10 Pips, 15 Pips and so on.

D1 is the distance between N2 and N3. D1 should never be more than 15 Pips to qualify for entry.
D2 is distance between node N2 and the entry point. D2 should always be less than ten pips.

N2 should always equal or less than 20C low for Sell.
N2 should always equal or less than 20H High for Buy.

Condition: N4 shouldn't have long vick. Should be less than 5 pips. If significant candle is formed at N4 or till reaching TP, exit.

Significant candle is a candle that has wick height (equal height both sides) more than the size of the body.

Condition: Distance between the entry point and a Fibo line or MA50 or MA100 or MA200 should be minimum 20 pips.

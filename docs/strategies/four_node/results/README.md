## Why it fails

Gross (pre-cost) mean is between -10.3 and 9.5 bps per trade in discovery, against a 14.0 bps round trip. Stops sit one tick beyond N3, so the risk unit is small and costs consume more than the whole edge. Mean R is positive (about 0.1-0.2 R), but R does not pay for costs that are charged on notional.

## Variant code

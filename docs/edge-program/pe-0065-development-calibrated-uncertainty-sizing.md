# PE-0065 development-calibrated uncertainty sizing

Added a development-only calibration policy for standardized forecast errors. It accepts unique, dated `DEVELOPMENT` rows only, estimates an empirical upper-quantile error multiplier, never deflates the supplied uncertainty estimate, hashes the data and protocol, and freezes the result in a durable immutable registry before the validation start. The shared signal allocator can consume that frozen policy and per-name forecast standard errors; its uncertainty haircut reduces adjusted net alpha and is recorded in the allocation result. The forward paper/shadow runner can also require a policy identity, rejects sessions before validation starts, and applies a bounded haircut to candidate quantity before risk checks and paper intent creation. Missing forecast uncertainty or a haircut below one whole share produces a recorded no-trade. Sizing never increases a position to reach a profit target.

The package is `PARTIAL`: only synthetic fixtures were available, and a paper runner is forced to use the policy only when its operator supplies the registry. A real development sample, predeclared protocol review, and routine cohort wiring are still required before this can be treated as market-calibrated practice. Synthetic monotonicity and integration tests demonstrate software behavior, not calibration quality or trading performance.

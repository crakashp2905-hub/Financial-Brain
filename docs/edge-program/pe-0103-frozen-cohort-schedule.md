# PE-0103 — Frozen prospective cohort schedule

The protected cohort registry binds a schedule to an immutable experiment charter. The charter commits strategy, model, rights, data snapshot, market calendar, universe, costs, risk/stop policy, benchmarks, capital, parameters and horizon. Cohort edits after registration require a new version and later prospective start; closed outcomes remain immutable.

PE-0103 remains partial because schedules are date ranges rather than explicit exchange-session lists resolved from an authoritative frozen calendar, and no prospective cohort has yet started.

Evidence and source hashes are in `pe-0103-frozen-cohort-schedule-evidence.json`.

# Quote quality and execution gate (PE-0040)

The paper execution adapter accepts only a valid, two-sided displayed quote. It rejects crossed or locked books, non-positive or missing prices, zero displayed quantity, stale or future-stamped evidence, spreads above the frozen model limit, zero/invalid reported volume, exchange halt/circuit indicators, circuit-band touches, active auction flags, and order quantities above a configured freeze limit. Rejected quote event hashes and reason codes are retained in the decision receipt. If quote candidates exist but all fail quality checks, the outcome is a durable no-trade; no favorable spread is interpolated.

Age is measured from the normalized event timestamp to local receipt. The feed-specific timestamp meaning matters: for Kite REST quotes this may not represent a depth-capture timestamp. The configured age bound is therefore an explicit paper-model policy, not a guarantee of source freshness. A deployment must choose and freeze a defensible source-specific bound before treating these quotes as executable evidence.

Kite's documented quote snapshot includes exchange timestamp, last-trade time, market depth, volume and lower/upper circuit limits. The REST quote fields do not establish auction phase. Auction rejection is enforced when an upstream normalized source supplies `auction_active` or `market_phase`; the current Kite polling adapter cannot independently prove auction state. Do not infer an inactive auction from a missing field. Wiring a trusted session/auction calendar or another authorized source remains open work.

Freeze quantities can be configured per instrument in the execution model, which freezes them into the model digest. Production callers must populate them from the effective-dated instrument/order-rule master; absent that integration, the quality gate cannot claim freeze-limit coverage. Quote simulation remains a conservative paper approximation, not a broker fill or proof of market execution.

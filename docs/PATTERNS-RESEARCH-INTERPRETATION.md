# Chart patterns: research interpretation

- Book statistics (Nison, Bulkowski) describe historical samples: US stocks in the 1990s, often confirmed cases only.
  They are conditional on that sample and selection. They are not Indian trade win rates.
- Confirmed-only success rates ignore failed and unconfirmed candidates. Always report the denominator, including them.
- Ultimate-high or ultimate-low bias: a pattern's best price is known only in hindsight. Never use it as an exit.
- Multiplicity: every variant counts. The family is one collective hypothesis, not 72 independent claims.
- A result that matches a placebo or a momentum baseline is not evidence of pattern-specific information.
- Synthetic corpora test code paths. They say nothing about markets.
- The 2026Q4 trial budget is spent (13/13). Protected histories and the holdout are not re-tested here.

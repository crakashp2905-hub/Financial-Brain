## W1 finding and contract

`champion.standings()` previously used every `horizon`-th **observed** cohort row. A missing date could shift later selections. It also included `complete IS NULL` legacy records in the pivot. The revised evaluator accepts a frozen list of scheduled rebalance dates and separately counts missing, legacy, incomplete, non-executable, unverified, and diagnostic pairs. No schedule means zero promotion-grade cohorts. Independent eligibility remains a separate input and defaults to none. New picks reject missing/duplicate security lineages before writing; duplicate-lineage aliases in old picks and multiply matched forward-price rows are unresolved rather than double-counted or last-row-wins. Historical ledger rows and the protected holdout were not rewritten. Results from the existing `resolve()` are explicitly versioned `ranking_close_v1` and remain close-to-close ranking diagnostics, not executable next-session P&L; only a separately implemented `executable_next_session_v1` metric could enter promotion inference.

The W1 patch does **not** yet prove the schedule is a real exchange calendar or that it was committed before outcomes. It does not resolve corporate actions, duplicate lineages, benchmark missingness, cost-unit audit, or next-session fills. Therefore it cannot grant `EDGE_CONFIRMED` or live-order eligibility.
